Munich Germany

Living in Munich, he built a life of his own.
A new city, far from home, new routines, new people — and slowly, a place that once felt far away started feeling a little like home.

There were long days, good days, a lot of figuring things out, and plenty of memories along the way.

And somewhere in the middle of all that, there was her.

Calls that lasted longer than they should, visits that always felt too short, and a lot of wishing she was there.

Munich became a pretty big part of his story.
But he never knew that the person on the other side of the distance would eventually make him want to come home.

Bangalore India

And then there was Bangalore.
Her home. His home too.

She had her own little world here — her people, her memories, her favourite places, and all the things that made this city hers.

He had grown up with his own memories of Bangalore too, never imagining that somewhere in the same city, another story was unfolding.

Two people, living their own lives, in the same city, without knowing they would one day become each other's story.

Then they met.

And suddenly, Bangalore wasn't just the city they came from.
It became the place where everything began.

After all the distance, all the different chapters, and all the roads that brought them here — this is where they get to build their life together.
